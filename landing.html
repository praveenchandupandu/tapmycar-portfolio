How it works

Simple as tap, call, done.

No app install required for the person contacting you. Works on every phone.

1

Register and get your free eTag

Sign up with your phone number. Get a free QR code instantly — no credit card needed.

2

Place your tag on your car

Print and place your QR code on your windshield or bumper. Physical NFC sticker ships on activation.

3

Strangers scan and call you

Anyone with a phone can scan your QR and tap Call. The call routes through our private network — your real number stays hidden.

4

You screen every call first

You hear an automated voice first before speaking to the stranger. Press 1 to send an auto-message. Press 2 to connect directly.
