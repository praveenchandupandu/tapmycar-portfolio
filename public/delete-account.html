Delete your account

TapMyCar is operated by Praman Tech LLC (Connecticut, USA).

You can permanently delete your TapMyCar account and personal data at any time, using either method below.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

Open the TapMyCar app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account, and confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Option 2 — By email

Email support@tapmycar.io from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We verify the request and complete the deletion for you.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and profile (name, email, phone number, emergency contact).
  • Your tags are disabled and unlinked from you.
  • Any active subscription is cancelled (and refunded if you are within your eligible refund window, per our refund policy).
  • Scan history, push-notification tokens, and any messages, photos, or voice notes tied to your tags.

What we keep, and why

We retain basic transaction and order records (for example, that a purchase or refund occurred, and when) where we are legally required to for accounting, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes. Personal information in those records is minimized. These records are not used to contact or profile you after deletion.

How long it takes

In-app deletion is immediate. Email requests are completed promptly, and in all cases within 30 days. Refunds, where due, are returned to your original payment method and typically arrive within 5–10 business days.
